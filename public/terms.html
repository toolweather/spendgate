Terms

Advisory, and only for the score.

These terms cover the SpendGate software as deployed by whoever runs this Worker. They are a plain-language stub, not a negotiated contract.

What you get

A deterministic score and a verdict: approve, ask_human, or block. That is decision support for an agent. It is not a payment, not custody, not insurance, and not a promise that a merchant, chain, or counterparty will behave.

What you still do

You choose whether to pay. If the verdict is ask_human, stop and ask a person. If it is block, do not pay. Passing known_merchant or human_preapproved is your claim. SpendGate does not verify it.

Pro waitlist

SpendGate Pro is a demand test at $19 per month for teams. Joining the waitlist stores an email address. It does not create an account, start a subscription, or charge a card. No email is sent during the test.

Paid calls

After the free daily cap, a score can require a USDC payment on Base via x402. That payment is for the score request. It is separate from any amount you later send to a merchant. Settlement is handled by the PayAI facilitator configured for this deployment. If PAY_TO is unset, paid scores are refused.

No warranty

The service is provided as available, on a free Cloudflare plan unless the operator upgrades it. Scoring rules can change with the policy version. Do not rely on SpendGate as your only budget control. Keep a hard cap in the agent.

Acceptable use

Do not use the API to probe for a way around your own limits, to harass the facilitator, or to move money you are not allowed to move. Sanctions and fraud screening are not part of v1. That responsibility stays with the caller.
