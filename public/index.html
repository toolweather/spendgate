How v1 decides

Higher spend, lower score

The amount penalty is min(75, round(25 × log10(1 + usd))). A missing or unknown merchant lowers the score further. Known hosts on /v1/policy stay known with no network call. Other domains, https URLs, and 0x payees are checked with PayCheck. If PayCheck is down, the local merchant rules still apply.

Category risk

crypto_transfer, irreversible, and gift_card are high. api_call, saas, and infra are medium. status_check is low. Anything else is unknown.

Two hard rules

Over a budget you pass in, the verdict is block. A PayCheck block is also do not pay. Strictly under $1, it is approve, unless one of those block rules fires first.

Call it from an agent

curl -s $ORIGIN/v1/score \
  -H 'content-type: application/json' \
  -d '{"amount":12,"currency":"USDC","category":"api_call","merchant":"https://api.openai.com","agent_id":"planner"}'
